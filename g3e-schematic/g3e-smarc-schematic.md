# RZ/G3E SMARC Module — SoC IO pin map (sheets 15–17)

## 0. How to read this file

- **Port**: RZ/G3E GPIO port name (first field of the pin-mux string).
- **Ball**: BGA ball of U1 (RZ/G3E).
- **Net**: net name on the schematic. `A → B` means a series resistor separates SoC-side net A from module-side net B.
- **Dir**: direction of the off-page connector as drawn (`IN`/`OUT`/`BI`) — this is the schematic designer's intent, *not* a pinctrl setting.
- **Off-page xref**: sheet+zone where the net continues (sheets not included in this extract).
- **Full pin-mux list**: copied as printed on the symbol (including the typo `Resreved`). The function actually used is the one implied by the net name.

## 5. Notes relevant to PMOD / device-tree work

1. **SMARC `SPI1_*` lands on PM4–PM7**: `SPI1_DIN`=PM4 (AA3), `SPI1_DO`=PM5 (AB2), `SPI1_CK`=PM6 (AA1), `SPI1_CS0#`=PM7 (AA2). The net names only say `SPI1_*` (SMARC naming); the pin-mux list offers two SPI-capable peripherals on these pins: the **RSPI "A" pin set** (MISOA/MOSIA/RSPCKA/SSLA0) and **SCI8** (`RXD8_MISO8_SCL8`, `TXD8_MOSI8_SDA8`, `SCK8`, `SS8_CTS8N_RTS8N`), so a DT can pinmux these pins either to the RSPI or to SCI8 (SPI/simple-SPI mode). Only one can own the pins at a time.
2. `I2C_GP_CK/DAT` = P32/P33 (RIIC1), 2K2 pull-ups to 1.8V_AWO on the module. This bus goes to the carrier (audio codec DA7212 and PMOD1 Type-6A via level shifter IC25).
3. `GPIO4` = P31 (AJ4), `GPIO5` = P36 (AH3): these are the PMOD0 pins 7/8 (INT/RESET) on the carrier.
4. Not on these sheets (need other module sheets): SMARC `GPIO6`, `GPIO7`, `GPIO8`–`GPIO13`, `SER0_*`, `POWER_BTN#`, `SLEEP#`, `I2S0_*`, `AUDIO_MCK`. Their SoC port cannot be determined from this extract.
5. Off-page sheets referenced (not included): 2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25.
